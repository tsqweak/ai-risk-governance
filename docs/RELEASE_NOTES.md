# Release Notes

## v0.6.0

v0.6.0 establishes the AI-Risk-Governance platform as a bank-grade governance demonstration spanning traditional governance, AI governance, and agentic AI governance.

### Major Capabilities Delivered

- AI System Registry for governed AI systems, including ownership, lifecycle, risk tier, jurisdictions, components, controls, evidence, and audit history.
- Regulatory Mapping Engine linking regulations, requirements, controls, evidence expectations, and AI systems.
- Continuous Control Monitoring Engine with test execution, test runs, findings, approved exceptions, and governance explanations.
- Monitoring dashboards for control health, findings, exceptions, AI-system monitoring, and portfolio risk heatmaps.
- Evidence governance with evidence requirements, evidence objects, health, validation, expiration awareness, and control/regulatory traceability.
- Executive and auditor experiences, including portfolio views, regulatory coverage, governance committee views, auditor workspace, and board-style reporting.
- AI Governance Framework covering models, prompts, agents, delegated authority, tool permissions, human oversight, and AI risk domains.
- Agentic AI Controls covering agentic levels, permitted actions, tool governance, approval requirements, execution logs, kill switches, and escalation.

### Completed Phases

- Phase 1 - AI System Registry
- Phase 2 - Regulatory Mapping Engine
- Phase 3A - Continuous Control Monitoring Engine
- Phase 3B - Monitoring Dashboards & Control Health
- Phase 4 - Evidence Objects & Evidence Governance
- Phase 5 - Executive & Auditor Experience
- Phase 6A - AI Governance Framework
- Phase 6B - Agentic AI Controls

### GitHub Migration Milestone

v0.6.0 marks the project migration into GitHub-backed source control, creating a durable baseline for future documentation, roadmap, governance workflow, and platform implementation work.

### Audience Impact

- IT Risk can review AI system inventory, control health, findings, and exceptions.
- Internal Audit can trace requirements to controls, evidence, and audit-ready records.
- Compliance can inspect regulatory coverage and gaps across jurisdictions.
- AI Governance Committees can review model, prompt, agent, authority, oversight, and risk-domain posture.
- CIO/CRO leadership can consume executive-ready portfolio and board reporting views.
- Regulators can follow explainable mappings from supervisory expectations to governed AI systems and evidence.
